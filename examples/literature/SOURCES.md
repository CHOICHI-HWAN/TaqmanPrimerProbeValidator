# Literature examples — sources and licences

`nipah.tsv` in this folder lists primer/probe sets that were **published in the papers below**. They are included only as working
examples for the "📚 Literature primers" menu (so the menu and "add to analysis" can be tried without any private data). Each paper
allows redistribution under the licence given; credit is given here, in the `citation`/`doi`/`license` columns of the file, and on the
dashboard.

| paper | licence | link |
|---|---|---|
| Sendow I, Ratnawati A, Taylor T, et al. Nipah virus in the fruit bat *Pteropus vampyrus* in Sumatera, Indonesia. PLoS ONE 2013;8(7):e69544 | CC BY (Creative Commons Attribution License) | <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0069544> |
| Jensen KS, Adams R, Bennett RS, et al. Development of a novel real-time polymerase chain reaction assay for the quantitative detection of Nipah virus replicative viral RNA. PLoS ONE 2018;13(6):e0199534 | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) | <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0199534> |
| Ranadheera C, Proulx R, Chaiyakul M, et al. The interaction between the Nipah virus nucleocapsid protein and phosphoprotein regulates virus replication. Sci Rep 2018;8:15994 | CC BY 4.0 | <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-34484-7> |

**Changes made:** the oligonucleotide sequences were copied from the text of each paper into a tab-separated table, without altering them;
the columns for the target, the verification note and the remark were added by this project. Nothing else of the papers is reproduced.

**What this is not:** the sequences are given as published, for research use only. They are not an endorsement or a validated diagnostic
protocol, and this project has not checked whether any of them is covered by a patent or other rights. Whether an assay detects a
particular virus variant has to be established by sequence comparison (this tool) and by experiment.

Mark an entry as "원문 대조 완료" (verified against the paper) only after comparing the sequences with the paper itself and noting where.
